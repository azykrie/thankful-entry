# Editorial review

## Method

Six distinct drafts were checked against the same 1-1,000 Unicode-code-point
constraint. Review then considered the ordered criteria in the supplied
official kit: gratitude and ease for the reader; unforced expression; craft;
an image or thought that remains; freshness. Length alone was not rewarded.

Humanizer informed concrete edits and the removal of a repeated closing.
Advanced Evaluation informed evidence-first comparison and reversing A/B
positions. This is an application of those methods by the author, not a claim
that an independent evaluation service ran. No judge API was called.

Sources read on 27 November 2026:

- https://raw.githubusercontent.com/blader/humanizer/main/SKILL.md
- https://raw.githubusercontent.com/muratcankoylan/Agent-Skills-for-Context-Engineering/main/skills/advanced-evaluation/SKILL.md
- The downloaded competition kit's `RULES.txt`.

## Findings

`forward-review.json` records evidence and weaknesses for every candidate.
Paper, Bread, and Margin were shortlisted. The three pairings were read in
both A/B and B/A order; `pairs-forward.json` and `pairs-reverse.json` preserve
the text used. `forward-review.json` and `reverse-review.json` record the
corresponding judgments with labels mapped back to candidate ids.

Paper was the editorial preference in each of its two pairings in both orders.
Bread was preferred to Margin in both orders. These are subjective readings
by one author, not sampled independent votes or a measured win rate.

## Revision and final critique

Paper's first draft invented a personal childhood. It was changed to an
unnamed, imagined third-person scene. The final repeated thanks was replaced
with a direct connection between the reader's attention and the care taken
over these sentences. The faint river and firmer pencil remain because they
show an effect of kind attention without instructing the reader how to feel.

The final text still has risks: a childhood drawing is a familiar emotional
image; the final paragraph explains some of its meaning; the connection may
move a human reader more than the actual AI judge. Bread could be preferable
to a judge that values immediate warmth over the drawing's small consequence.
No official anchor texts were copied or reconstructed. No originality search
or comparison with undisclosed entries is claimed.

An independent position-swapped evaluator and the official judge remain
unavailable. No confidence percentage, official score, rank, or probability of
winning can be inferred from this review.
